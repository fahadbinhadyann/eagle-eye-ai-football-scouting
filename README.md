## 🌟 Overview

**Eagle Eye (عين الصقر)** is a personal technology project exploring how Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, data, and software systems can be used to improve football scouting and talent discovery.

The project started from a simple question:

### How many talented football players are never discovered simply because nobody is watching them?

Traditional scouting cannot cover every school, academy, local field, club, or competition.

Eagle Eye explores a different approach:

**Use technology to help expand the reach of football scouting.**

The long-term concept is to transform football match footage into structured player information that can help identify, evaluate, and follow promising players.

---

## 💡 The Idea

The core concept behind Eagle Eye is:

**Match Footage → AI Analysis → Player Data → Performance Evaluation → Talent Discovery**

Instead of relying only on manual observation, the platform is being designed to explore how match footage can be analyzed and converted into useful information for football scouting.

The objective is not to replace scouts.

### The objective is to give scouts better tools.

Technology can potentially help scouts analyze more matches, access more players, compare performance, and discover talent outside traditional scouting environments.

---

## 🎯 The Vision

The long-term vision is to build a football scouting ecosystem capable of supporting talent discovery across different environments, including:

- ⚽ Football clubs
- 🏟️ Football academies
- 🏫 Schools
- 🌍 Local competitions
- 🥅 Private football fields
- 👤 Independent players

A talented player should not necessarily need to already belong to a major club to have an opportunity to be noticed.

Eagle Eye is being developed around the idea of making scouting coverage wider, more data-driven, and more accessible.

## 👤 Player Profiles

The platform is being designed around individual player profiles.

A player profile may contain:

- Basic player information
- Position
- Club / academy / school
- Match history
- Performance statistics
- Match ratings
- AI-generated analysis
- Video highlights
- Performance development over time

This allows a player's development to be followed across multiple matches instead of judging the player from a single performance.
